# Roadmap

## Roadmap Philosophy

BIGGIEYES is being developed as a protocol, not a single campaign mint. The roadmap therefore prioritizes infrastructure quality, transparency, and sustainable tokenomics before aggressive expansion.

## Phase 1. Foundation

**Status:** Implemented in the current repository footprint

- core NFT collection with ticket mint and VRF redeem flow
- public collection architecture
- BIGGI token with capped strategic allocation
- distributor, reserve, treasury, buyback, drip, and liquidity branches
- frontend panels for tokenomics, VRF, rewards, user views, and community views
- reader contracts and deployment registries

## Phase 2. Hardening

**Status:** In progress

- production-grade deployment manifests
- broader automated monitoring and incident alerting
- parameter review for reward emissions and DEX thresholds
- tighter keeper and policy operational controls
- explorer verification and release documentation per deployment

## Phase 3. Mainnet Readiness

**Target outcomes**

- migrate from public testnet posture to mainnet governance standards
- multisig ownership across all privileged contracts
- finalized treasury and reserve operating policy
- final public documentation portal and launch-grade investor materials
- operational dashboards for treasury, reserve, buybacks, and VRF fulfillment

## Phase 4. Ecosystem Expansion

**Target outcomes**

- additional collections connected through the distributor and token rewards rails
- expanded narrative layers for the BiggiVerse
- richer community programs and event-driven grants
- partner drops and branded collection collaborations
- secondary-market intelligence and collector tooling

## Phase 5. Governance Evolution

**Target outcomes**

- formalize parameter governance
- move owner-managed settings into multisig or governance processes
- publish transparent change logs for economic parameter updates
- create public operating standards for treasury, reserve, and reward policy

## Phase 6. Market Infrastructure

**Target outcomes**

- advanced liquidity analytics and LP reporting
- buyback policy tuning by market regime
- treasury and reserve dashboards for on-chain observers
- improved execution tooling for liquidity and drip automation

## Phase 7. Community Layer Maturity

**Target outcomes**

- deeper event and campaign tooling in `CommunityCenter`
- stronger governance-aware reward programs
- transparent community grant allocation reports
- creator and collector participation loops tied to protocol health

## Milestone Themes

### Technical

- optimize reliability
- improve observability
- reduce operational centralization
- preserve reader compatibility across releases

### Economic

- maintain supply discipline
- make buybacks productive
- keep rewards capped and transparent
- strengthen reserve-backed liquidity operations

### Product

- make mint and redeem flows intuitive
- surface protocol transparency directly in the UI
- make collection progression and reward eligibility easy to understand

## Long-Term Vision

BIGGIEYES aims to become a collectible ecosystem where:

- NFT minting is verifiably fair
- tokenomics are inspectable on-chain
- revenue is transparently recycled into protocol growth
- community participation is funded by visible protocol activity

That is the strategic direction behind every roadmap phase.
