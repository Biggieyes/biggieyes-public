# Investor Overview

## Investment Thesis

BIGGIEYES sits at the intersection of three durable Web3 categories:

- collectible NFTs
- on-chain reward economies
- protocol-owned liquidity and treasury management

The opportunity is not just a single collection launch. It is a reusable infrastructure model for NFT ecosystems where mint revenue is transparently recycled into holder utility, reserve depth, market support, and community growth.

## What Makes BIGGIEYES Different

Most NFT projects stop at mint, reveal, and secondary-market storytelling. BIGGIEYES extends the economic lifecycle:

- tickets are tradable before reveal
- Chainlink VRF makes redemption verifiably fair
- mint revenue is automatically split into protocol destinations
- BIGGI creates a visible token utility layer
- buybacks, treasury routing, and reserve-backed liquidity tie market activity back to holders

This gives BIGGIEYES a stronger retention and ecosystem design than a standard PFP or reveal collection.

## Current Product State

The current repository and contract stack already include:

- a ticket-based main collection
- a public mint companion collection
- a deployed Polygon mainnet address registry
- a Chainlink VRF router integration
- a full routing stack for reserve, treasury, buyback, drip, and liquidity modules
- a live frontend architecture with tokenomics, VRF, rewards, user, and community panels

This means BIGGIEYES is already built as a protocol system rather than a slide-deck concept.

## Economic Engine

BIGGIEYES embeds value routing into the protocol itself.

### Gross mint routing

- 60% of mint value goes to the protocol distributor
- 40% goes to the development wallet

### Protocol routing

- collection rewards
- reserve liquidity support
- buyback execution
- treasury accumulation
- community grants and events

This creates a visible treasury logic that investors, users, and partners can inspect.

## Token Framework

BIGGI has a hard max supply of `1,000,000,000`, with current strategic caps allocated to:

- reserve
- drip distributor
- token rewards

The token is therefore not inflationary by default. Emissions are bounded and tied to clear utility paths.

## Why Polygon

BIGGIEYES is built for Polygon because the network supports:

- low-cost NFT and reward interactions
- user-friendly collector onboarding
- strong compatibility with Chainlink infrastructure
- practical DeFi integration for liquidity and buybacks

The active repo registry targets Polygon mainnet for live protocol operation.

## Revenue And Value Loops

BIGGIEYES has several reinforcing loops:

1. mint activity creates protocol inflow
2. inflow strengthens rewards, reserve, treasury, buybacks, and community funding
3. buybacks recycle value into BIGGI
4. treasury redistributes BIGGI across key ecosystem rails
5. collectors receive recurring utility through rewards and completion claims

This is the foundation of a compounding NFT economy rather than a one-time launch event.

## Competitive Advantages

## Risk View

The main risks are typical for early-stage Web3 infrastructure:

- admin centralization before governance hardening
- operational complexity across many contracts
- dependence on DEX liquidity quality and external automation
- need for disciplined parameter management during launch and scale

These are reducible risks, and the current architecture already addresses them through contract separation, caps, pending accounting, and policy guardrails.
