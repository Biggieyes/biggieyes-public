Why this topic matters

What BIGGI is used for

BIGGI is not only a token on a chart. It powers weekly NFT rewards and supports the wider ecosystem as the project grows.

What treasury and reserve do

Treasury routes value into clear branches such as rewards, reserve, and drip, while reserve acts as a protective layer when the ecosystem needs support.

Why liquidity and buyback matter

Liquidity keeps BIGGI tradable on DEX markets, and buyback plus guardrails are designed to help the system respond more safely in stressed conditions.

Why this page exists

Most visitors need the ecosystem explained before raw on-chain numbers become meaningful. This page gives that context first, while the app remains the live operational view.

Key points

  • BIGGI rewards, treasury, reserve, drip, buyback, and liquidity are part of one connected system.
