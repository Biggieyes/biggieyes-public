BiggiEyes Collection

BiggiEyes is not built as one flat drop. It uses chapters and blocks so the collection can grow over time while keeping pricing, rarity, and rewards organized. This page explains that structure before you enter the live app grid.

Open Collection In App See VRF Mint Flow

Collection signals that matter

Why chapters matter

New collection releases can be added without rebuilding the whole system, which makes future expansion much cleaner.

What blocks actually do

Blocks help organize pricing and reward context so every mint has a known place inside the wider collectible map.

What collectors can hold

Before reveal you own the ticket. After redeem and randomness, you own the final NFT. Both states belong to the same public collectible flow.

Why this page exists

Most visitors first need to understand how the collection is built. This page gives them a stable explanation before they enter the live dashboard and inspect real-time collection state.

Key points

  • The collection is built for future chapter expansion, not a one-time drop.
  • Tickets come first, then redeem into final NFTs through the VRF flow.
  • Collection structure later affects rarity and reward context inside the app.
