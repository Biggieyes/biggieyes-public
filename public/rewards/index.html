What this page covers

What holders actually do

Hold the finalized NFT, open the rewards panel, and claim BIGGI when that week's window is available.

Why payouts are different

Claim size is not flat. Rarity tier and collection weight can increase the BIGGI attached to a stronger NFT.

Why the result feels credible

Rewards read from the same on-chain collection state as the reveal, so the payout stays tied to the NFT you actually own.

Why this page exists

This page explains the reward mechanic before a visitor ever opens a wallet-connected dashboard.

Once someone understands that finalized NFTs can claim BIGGI weekly and that rarity can change the amount, the app becomes the right place to check wallet eligibility, live status, and claim timing.

Key points

  • Finalized NFTs can unlock the weekly BIGGI claim flow.
  • Rarity tier and collection weight can increase the payout.
  • The app shows live eligibility, claim timing, and wallet state.

Rewards FAQ

What can claim BIGGI?

Finalized NFTs can claim BIGGI once they are eligible after the reveal process is complete.

How often?

Each eligible NFT can claim once per week through the live app flow.

Why does rarity matter?

Rarity tier and collection weight change the weekly claim amount, so stronger outcomes can carry more reward weight.
