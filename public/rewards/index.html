What this page covers

Weekly claim cycle

Rewards are not a permanent passive counter. Each eligible finalized NFT can claim BIGGI once per week through the live app flow.

Rarity-weighted amount

Claim size is tied to block weight and rarity tier, so stronger revealed outcomes carry more weekly BIGGI than lower-weight ones.

Completed-set context

Rewards stay connected to collection progress. Finalized NFTs, set completion, and reward context all sit on the same on-chain state instead of a separate loyalty layer.

Why the rewards page matters for search

A focused rewards page gives search engines a stable topic URL for collection rewards, while the app panel remains the live operational view for connected users.

This split is useful because a search visitor usually wants to understand the mechanic first. Once they know that finalized NFTs claim BIGGI weekly and rarity changes the amount, the app panel becomes the place to check eligibility, wallet state, and live claim data.

Key points

  • Only eligible finalized NFTs enter the weekly BIGGI claim flow.
  • Claim amount depends on block weight and rarity tier.
  • Rewards stay tied to collection completion and VRF reveal outcomes.
