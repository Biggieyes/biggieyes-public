Ticket To Reveal

BiggiEyes VRF Mint Flow

BiggiEyes starts with a tradable ticket, not a final NFT. The ticket starts at 500 POL, rises by 0.33% after every mint, and only becomes the final NFT after the redeem flow triggers verifiable randomness.

Flow overview

Step 1: Enter through the ticket

The first asset is the ticket itself, and its price moves on a public curve after every new mint.

Step 2: Redeem into randomness

Redeem triggers verifiable randomness so the final result is not chosen by a team wallet or private reveal list.

Step 3: Hold the final asset

After the reveal is complete, the NFT becomes the asset used by the collection, rarity, and weekly reward logic.

Why this matters

Most visitors need the mint flow explained before they open a wallet panel. This page gives them the process in plain language, while the app remains the live place for mint and redeem actions.

Key points

  • The ticket starts at 500 POL and rises by 0.33% after every mint.
  • Tickets remain tradable before the reveal step is completed.
  • Verifiable randomness determines the final NFT outcome used by collection and rewards logic.

VRF Mint FAQ

What is minted first?

A tradable ticket is minted first, not the final NFT.

How does pricing move?

The ticket starts at 500 POL and rises by 0.33% after every mint.

When is the NFT final?

The NFT becomes final only after the redeem step finishes the reveal outcome through verifiable randomness.
