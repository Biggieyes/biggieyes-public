Ticket To Reveal

BiggiEyes VRF Mint Flow

BiggiEyes starts with a tradable ticket, not a final NFT. The ticket starts at 500 POL, rises by 0.33% after every mint, and only becomes the final NFT after the VRF redeem flow assigns the on-chain reveal outcome.

Flow overview

Ticket price curve

The entry asset starts at 500 POL and rises by 0.33% after every mint, so the ticket has its own price curve before reveal.

VRF redeem step

Redeem triggers the randomness path that determines the final outcome instead of relying on a manual reveal table.

Final NFT state

After VRF completion, the ticket becomes the final NFT and can then be evaluated in the collection and rewards context.

Why this matters

Searchers looking for VRF NFT mint mechanics generally want a direct explanation of the process. Key points
