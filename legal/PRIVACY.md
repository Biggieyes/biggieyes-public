# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-14

This policy describes how data is handled by a hosted BiggiEyes instance. If you self host, you control the data and are responsible for your own policy.

## Data we collect
- Wallet addresses and public on-chain activity.
- Chat messages, usernames, and timestamps if chat is enabled.
- Server logs such as IP address, user agent, and request metadata.
- Support communications you send to us.

## How we use data
- Provide the app and its features.
- Prevent abuse and moderate chat.
- Improve reliability and security.

## Sharing
We may share data with infrastructure providers that run the service:
- RPC providers for blockchain access.
- Supabase for chat storage and realtime.
- Netlify for hosting and serverless functions.
- Pinata and optional NFT.Storage for IPFS pinning.

These providers process data under their own policies.

## Retention
- On-chain data is public and permanent.
- Chat data is stored until it is deleted by moderation or retention rules.
- Server logs are retained for a limited time for security and debugging.

## Your choices
- Do not use chat if you do not want your messages stored.
- Use your own self hosted instance for full control.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact privacy@biggieyes.com.
