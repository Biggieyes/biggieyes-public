How it works

Ticket -> Redeem -> VRF -> NFT. You mint a Ticket first, then redeem it. VRF selects the final mutation, and the Ticket turns into the final NFT.

Five Chapters

The series has five chapters. Each chapter starts with a VRF phase of 550 Tickets. Public mint opens only after that VRF set sells out. Public pricing follows the block prices formed by the VRF outcome.

Tradable Tickets & NFTs

Tickets are tradable before redeem, and revealed NFTs are tradable after redeem. Tickets do not grant extra perks or weekly rewards. Ticket price increases after each mint, and value can also rise from reveal potential when collectors seek missing pieces for Collection Rewards.

Last Episode

The finale includes a $500,000 meta reward and allocates part of proceeds to a transparent charity account supporting children with visual impairments.

On-Chain Economics And Verification

BiggiEyes is built around a ticket-first model: every mint starts as a tradable Ticket, then becomes a final NFT after VRF redeem. Ticket pricing is read from smart contracts and updates with protocol activity, so users can verify how entry cost changes over time.

The revealed NFT state is not decided manually. VRF randomness and on-chain data decide final block/background outcomes, and those outcomes are reused by other mechanics such as rarity weighting and rewards. This makes the ecosystem deterministic from contract state and auditable in explorers.

BIGGI token flows, reserve/treasury balances, and panel-level statistics are part of the same public state. The landing page exposes live entry metrics, and the app panels provide deeper operational views for REWARDS, COLLECTION, VRF, BIGGI ECOSYSTEM, USERS, and COMMUNITY CENTER.
