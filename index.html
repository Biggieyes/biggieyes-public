How BiggiEyes Works

The main collector flow has three steps. Public Collection minting is a separate route that opens after all 550 tickets in the same chapter have been minted.

1

Get a Chapter Ticket

Get a chapter ticket or acquire an existing one. The ticket is your tradable entry to a verifiable random reveal.

2

Redeem Through VRF

Redeem burns the ticket and requests Chainlink randomness, which selects one still-available result from the chapter.

3

Own, Trade, or Use the NFT

The final NFT is sent to your wallet. Eligible NFTs can be traded, used for weekly BIGGI, or combined into reward sets.

Project Structure

BiggiEyes is deployed on Polygon Mainnet and organized into sequential chapters. Only one chapter opens at a time; the next one follows after the previous chapter's ticket supply is complete.

Inside Every Chapter

Two linked ways to collect Biggi NFTs

550 VRF NFTs revealed randomly from redeemed chapter tickets. 100 selectable Public NFTs with ten artworks in each eye-color block and no background clones. Public mint unlocks only after all 550 chapter tickets are minted. Public NFT price is read from the paired VRF collection's current eye-block price.

Connected Utility

What the NFT connects to

Weekly BIGGI claims use the owned NFT's eye-color block weight and the active weekly budget. Funded Collection Rewards motivate holders to assemble specific VRF NFT sets. Community events can assign claimable prizes to selected wallets. Treasury, reserve, drip, buyback, and liquidity contracts provide the tokenomics infrastructure.

BIGGI Tokenomics

How the BIGGI token connects collecting, rewards, and protocol liquidity

BIGGI is the capped ERC-20 ecosystem token of BiggiEyes on Polygon. It links eligible NFT rewards with reserve, drip, buyback, treasury, and liquidity modules. It is utility and accounting infrastructure, not a promise of profit or a guaranteed fixed return.

2.2B BIGGI Global token contract cap
1.2B BIGGI Initial allocation design across four defined destinations
1 EVM week Claim cycle for each eligible NFT

Initial BIGGI allocation

The token contract initializes four explicit ecosystem balances totaling 1.2 billion BIGGI.

600M Reserve and liquidity support 200M Token Rewards 200M Drip Distributor 200M Marketing Support

Hard limits and refill controls

The remaining supply is not an uncapped reward printer. Every mint remains below the 2.2B global cap.

DEX and drip refill authority has its own 500M BIGGI cumulative cap. Token Rewards refill authority has a separate 500M BIGGI cumulative cap. Guardian refills can be paused and are tracked independently on-chain. BIGGI is burnable, pausable, and supports permit approvals.
1

Collection activity

Supported ticket and Public Collection mints create POL inflow and can also expose configured BIGGI payment paths.

2

Transparent routing

Forty percent of native mint value goes to development and sixty percent enters the ecosystem distributor.

3

Buyback recycling

Bought BIGGI is routed through Treasury: 34% to Token Rewards, 33% to Reserve, and 33% to Drip.

4

Budgeted NFT rewards

Eligible NFTs contribute 10 to 100 units by eye-color block. The weekly controller limits payouts to the active budget.

Rewards Guide

Eligible NFTs connect to weekly BIGGI and funded collection challenges

VRF and Public Collection NFTs can claim BIGGI once per EVM week. The contract calculates units from the NFT's eye-color block weight, then applies the active weekly budget. A separate POL reward system recognizes specific completed sets from VRF collections.

VRF and Public NFTs can be eligible Higher eye blocks carry more reward units Separate funded POL set rewards

What the holder does

Hold an eligible NFT, open the Rewards panel, review the preview, and claim when the current weekly window permits it.

How weekly BIGGI is calculated

Each eye-color block has a weight from 10 to 100 units. The active weekly budget can reduce the default amount, but cannot raise it above the configured maximum.

How collection rewards differ

Collection Rewards use current ownership of qualifying VRF sets. A chapter must be fully funded before claims open, and each target can pay only once.

VRF Mint Guide

Entry begins with a tradable ticket and ends with a verifiable NFT reveal

A user mints or acquires a chapter ticket, then redeems it. The ticket is burned before the randomness request, and Chainlink VRF selects one still-unminted position from that chapter's 550-item matrix.

Tradable ticket before redeem Ticket is burned at redeem Retry path for a delayed VRF request

Step 1: Enter through the ticket

Your first asset is the ticket itself. Paid ticket mints use the current TicketHub price, which increases by the configured curve after each paid mint.

Step 2: Redeem into randomness

Redeem burns the ticket and requests Chainlink VRF so the normal result is not selected from a private off-chain list.

Step 3: Hold the final asset

After fulfillment, the selected NFT is minted to the redeemer. Delayed requests have a contract retry path after the configured delay.

BIGGI Tokenomics Guide

BIGGI connects NFT rewards with the wider tokenomics system

BIGGI is the ecosystem token used by weekly NFT rewards and as an alternative settlement asset for supported mints. Treasury, reserve, drip, buyback, and liquidity contracts separate the main financial responsibilities into visible on-chain modules.

Weekly NFT rewards use BIGGI Mint value follows fixed routing rules Reserve, drip, buyback, and liquidity are separate modules

What BIGGI is used for

BIGGI funds weekly rewards for eligible NFT holders and can be used by supported TicketHub and Public Collection mint paths.

What treasury and reserve mean

Treasury redistributes received BIGGI to Token Rewards, Reserve, and Drip, while Reserve keeps a separately accounted support balance.

Why liquidity and buyback matter

Liquidity provides the DEX market route for BIGGI. Buyback and guard contracts define controlled market-support actions rather than promising a price or return.

FAQ

How does minting start?

Get or acquire a chapter ticket, then redeem it. The ticket is burned and Chainlink VRF selects one still-available NFT from the chapter.

What do NFT holders get every week?

Eligible VRF and Public Collection NFTs can claim BIGGI once per EVM week. The amount is based on eye-color block weight and the active weekly reward budget; it is not a guaranteed fixed return.

What does VRF change?

VRF determines the final reveal outcome instead of a manual reveal list, which makes the result auditable from on-chain data.

Can collectors trade before and after reveal?

Yes. Tickets are tradable before redeem, and revealed NFTs remain tradable after the final state is assigned.

What is the Public Collection?

It is the paired 100-item collection for a chapter. Collectors choose an available artwork, there are no background clones, and minting opens only after all 550 tickets in that chapter are minted.

Is this homepage the full app?

No. This page is the public introduction. The interactive app is the separate Polygon Mainnet dashboard, and each transaction button reflects the current state of its deployed contract.
