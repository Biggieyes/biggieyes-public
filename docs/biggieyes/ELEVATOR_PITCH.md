# Elevator Pitch

## One-Sentence Pitch

BIGGIEYES is a Polygon NFT ecosystem where users mint tradable tickets, redeem them into Chainlink VRF-powered NFTs, and participate in a transparent on-chain economy powered by BIGGI rewards, treasury recycling, buybacks, and protocol-owned liquidity.

## 30-Second Pitch

BIGGIEYES combines collectible NFTs with real on-chain tokenomics. Users mint tickets, redeem them into random NFTs through Chainlink VRF, and every mint routes value into reserve, treasury, buybacks, collector rewards, and community funding. The result is a more transparent and sustainable NFT protocol, not just another reveal drop.

## 60-Second Pitch

BIGGIEYES is building a collectible protocol on Polygon. Instead of selling NFTs and stopping there, the system starts with tradable tickets, uses Chainlink VRF to assign NFTs fairly, and sends mint value through fixed on-chain rails into collection rewards, reserve liquidity, buybacks, treasury inventory, and community programs. BIGGI then ties the ecosystem together through weekly holder rewards and treasury recycling. It is a modular architecture that combines NFTs, token utility, and protocol-owned market support in one transparent stack.
