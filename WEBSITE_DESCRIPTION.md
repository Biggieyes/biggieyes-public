## Extended Website Description

BIGGIEYES is not just an NFT mint. It is a protocol-driven collectible ecosystem where every ticket mint contributes to on-chain reward pools, reserve liquidity, treasury growth, buybacks, and community participation. Users mint tickets, redeem them into random NFTs through Chainlink VRF, collect deeper trait sets, claim weekly BIGGI rewards, and track protocol health through live dashboards.

## Marketplace Description

The BIGGIEYES marketplace layer is designed around collectible progression and economic transparency. Tickets are tradable before reveal, final NFTs carry block and background identity, and collection completion has real value through native-token rewards. This creates a secondary market where rarity, completion strategy, and protocol activity all matter.

## Feature Section Copy

### Verifiable Randomness

Redeem tickets into NFTs through Chainlink VRF-backed randomness instead of opaque off-chain reveals.

### Protocol Tokenomics

Mint activity routes into reserve, treasury, buybacks, rewards, and community pools through fixed on-chain logic.

### Rewarded Collecting

Claim weekly BIGGI rewards and unlock native-token collection rewards by completing sets.

### Protocol-Owned Liquidity

Reserve and liquidity modules support long-term ecosystem structure rather than short-lived launch hype.
